# Why I built this

Everyone's tools sucked. They only filled one piece of a major puzzle. I pulled as many of those puzzle pieces together to build a better way for my agents to get the latest information. It's allowed me to do more with my agents by having them gather the latest and correct information by following standards of open research and science. All wrapped into an MCP that my agent can easily understand, instead of having to wire multiple tools into my agent: just have one, Ransack.

---

## Optional tightened variant (same ideas, fewer words)

Everyone's search tools filled one piece of the puzzle. I pulled the pieces together into one thing my agents can actually use: latest information, gathered to open-research standards, wrapped in a single MCP instead of five. One tool, Ransack.

## Optional closing line (ties the story to the receipts)

I hold Ransack to the same standard I want from it: there's a public benchmark in this repo that grades it against competitors, and it shows rows where we lose. Read it before you trust anything on this page.
